About

I am a Full Professor (Professeur des universités) of computer science at Université Lumière Lyon 2 (ICOM), where I am a member of the Learning and Decision research team (LeaD, ERIC laboratory). I head the first year of the work-study Master's programme in computer science.

My research lies at the intersection of networking and applied machine learning. I work on low-cost wireless sensor networks for monitoring physical phenomena, cooperative drone fleets for mobile sensing, energy-efficient machine learning for resource-constrained devices, and data-aware, goal-oriented communication.

From 2013 to 2025, I was Associate Professor at INSA Lyon (Telecommunications department, CITI laboratory, Inria Agora team), where I still teach as adjunct professor. I received my PhD in computer science from Université de Technologie de Compiègne in 2012 and my engineering degree from ESI Algiers in 2009, both with distinction.

Research

Current topics

  • Data-aware and goal-oriented communication. Adaptive access and communication schemes that account for the value of the data being sent.
  • UAV networks for monitoring highly dynamic pollution plumes. Spatio-temporal prediction architectures and reinforcement-learning-based path planning for drone fleets.
  • Energy-efficient neural networks for resource-constrained platforms. Evaluation and comparison of pruning, data selection and growing networks; new data selection methods.
  • Deployment and scheduling of sensor networks for air quality monitoring. Phenomenon-aware models for sensor placement and scheduling.
  • Low-cost sensor platforms for air quality monitoring. Deployed in Lyon (2018), on an onshore industrial site (since 2019) and on an offshore industrial site (since 2021).
